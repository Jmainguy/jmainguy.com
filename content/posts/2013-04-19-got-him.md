We got the terrorist alive, go America.

> **Editor's note, August 24, 2026:** This post was about the [capture of Dzhokhar Tsarnaev on April 19, 2013](https://www.cbsnews.com/boston/news/gunfire-in-watertown-minutes-after-police-scale-back-search/). He and his brother, Tamerlan Tsarnaev, carried out the Boston Marathon bombing four days earlier. I listened to the live police scanner over internet radio as officers found him hiding in a boat in Watertown, then wrote this post soon afterward in the excitement of the moment. [A July 2025 Associated Press report](https://apnews.com/article/adf1db19ee36dbfbc949da9e76218b63) covers the latest development in the legal fight over his death sentence.
