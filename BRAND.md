# jmainguy.com Brand Guide

## Purpose

jmainguy.com is Jonathan Mainguy's personal record of faith, family, software operations, open source work, leadership, and projects.

The site should feel like a personal field notebook crossed with an operator's manual: factual writing, working systems, direct convictions, and a clear record left for the people who follow.

## Voice

- Personal, direct, and specific.
- Faith is described as Jonathan's relationship with Jesus Christ, not reduced to a denominational label.
- Family members are named with care. Seth is always included with his siblings.
- Technical writing assumes curiosity and respects the reader.
- Leadership writing should focus on Jesus, servant leadership, and biblical principles.
- No em dashes in site chrome. No generic AI slogans or inflated claims.
- State the point directly. Avoid contrastive negation such as “not X, but Y,” “this isn't X, it's Y,” and “doesn't just X, it also Y.”

## Visual system

- **Blueprint ink** `#0B1F3A`: structure, rules, and primary text.
- **Blueprint paper** `#EDF4F8`: cool, readable background.
- **Deep teal** `#007C83`: labels and active details.
- **Blueprint blue** `#005EA8`: links, identity, and strong emphasis.
- **Light cyan** `#9BDDEA`: selected states, direct lineage, and important details.
- **Dark background** `#071629`, **dark panel** `#0D2947`, and **dark text** `#EDF8FC` preserve the same character in dark mode.

Use hard borders, utility labels, compressed display typography, and dense layouts that make good use of large screens. Imagery appears as evidence, not decoration.

The identity mark combines a living tree, a cross formed within its trunk, and software-like roots spreading toward connected nodes. It represents faith, family, open source work, and the different directions in which a life of service can grow. The mark does not rely on Jonathan's initials.

Use the Blueprint blue mark on light surfaces and the ice blue mark on dark surfaces. App icons retain the complete tree, cross, software roots, original colors, and transparent background. The favicon keeps the complete mark while using an ice-blue trunk and roots, teal and cyan leaves, and a Blueprint ink keyline. The keyline defines the mark on light browser chrome, while the ice blue remains visible on dark chrome. Chrome receives the full-resolution 1024-pixel favicon and performs the final downsample for the tab's display scale. The multi-size ICO remains available as a legacy root fallback.

## Site language

- Blog archive: **Logbook**
- Individual posts: **log entries**
- Personal values: **what matters to me**
- Projects: systems Jonathan **keeps online**
- Current state: **systems online**

The public family tree opens on Holden and Hazel and includes the complete eight-generation descendant chart researched by Mainguy.ca. Every documented person, spouse, parent, child, date, and location remains available through clickable relationships. Jonathan's branch continues beyond the 2007 chart through Leah Williams and their children: Seth, Cora "Cora Beth" Elizabeth, Deacon Ray, and Ezekiel Jay.

## Responsive requirements

- Fully usable at 360 × 616.
- Intentionally fills a 1920 × 1080 display.
- Minimum 44px controls.
- No horizontal overflow from long names or post titles.
